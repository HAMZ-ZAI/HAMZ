About Fat Pizza World

Discover the story behind your favorite pizza place!

Our Story

Fat Pizza World was founded in 2024 by two pizza enthusiasts, Fatuma and Aloy, who wanted to bring authentic Italian flavors to their community. Starting as a small food truck, we’ve grown into a beloved pizza chain known for our fresh ingredients and creative recipes.

Our Mission

We aim to make every pizza a masterpiece, using only the freshest ingredients and time-honored techniques. At Fat Pizza World, we believe pizza brings people together, and we’re here to create memorable dining experiences for all.
